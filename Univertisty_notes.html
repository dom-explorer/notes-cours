02 / Learning Philosophy

Understanding the mathematics behind the physics

Physics is not simply a collection of equations. Each equation expresses a relationship between measurable quantities, while mathematics provides a language for describing nature.

My notes emphasize step-by-step derivations, careful definitions, physical interpretations, dimensional analysis, worked examples, and connections between different mathematical representations.

When useful, the notes also incorporate diagrams, computational experiments, and interactive visualizations to make abstract concepts easier to explore.
